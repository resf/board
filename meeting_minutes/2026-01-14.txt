MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD

SUBJECT: Minutes for RESF Board Meeting Held January 14th, 2026

1. A meeting of the Board of Directiors (the "Board") of The Rocky Enterprise Software Foundation (the "Foundation") was held on January 14th, 2026 at 23:00 UTC, via video conference.

2. Attendance   

    a. The following directors, constituting a quorum, were present
        - Louis Abel
        - Benjamin Agner
        - Brian Clemens
        - Christopher DiBona
        - Mustafa Gezen
        - Taylor Goodwill
        - Neil Hanlon
        - Gregory Kurtzer
        - Sherif Nagy
        - Buyanjargal Shirnen
        - Christopher Stackpole
        - Theodore Tso

    b. The following directors were absent from the meeting:
        No directors were absent.

    c. Also present at the meeting was:
        - Michinori Nakahara, Advisor to the Board

3. Call to Order and Introductions

    Mr. Clemens called the meeting to order.

4. Board Discussion

    a. First, the Board approved the meeting agenda previously circulated to the Board by Mr. Clemens

    b. Next, the Board approved the minutes of the Board meeting held December 10th 2025.

    c. Next, Mr. Clemens provided an operations update, including the status of the Foundation's financials, upcoming events, and progress regarding the CIQ collaboration effort. Mr. Agner asked that Rocky Linux participate in the UNC Charlotte Cybersecurity Symposium, and expressed an intention to facilitate said participation.

    e. Next, Mr. Abel provided an update on the Rocky Linux Project. Mr. Abel discussed LookAhead builds, enabling continuous testing among other benefits.
    
    f. Next, Mr. Gezen, provided an update on the Peridot project.

    g. The Board discussed whether the Peridot project should remain a separate project under the RESF, or be folded into the Rocky Linux project. Noting that such changes are better postponed until the RESF's future restructuring, the former was agreed upon.
  
5. Adjournment

    There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at approximately 23:50 UTC.

Respectfully Submitted,
